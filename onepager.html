English

EEL (Evolution Evidence Ledger) — an immutable audit layer for the self-improving-AI era.

Problem: An AI that rewrites its own rules is most dangerous not when it errs, but when it errs and nobody can tell what changed or why.

Solution: A zero-dependency, append-only audit layer turning evolution into tamper-proof evidence:

  • Six Guardrails G1–G6: arXiv conclusions become enforceable rejections
  • Hash-Chain: edit any block → whole-chain verification fails, tampering exposed
  • Value Kernel: "good direction" as L0/L1/L2 vectors + hard constraints
  • PBFT Consensus: multi-agent witness signatures required

Why $3M: the only ledger unifying trust/transparency/value/evolution; six candidate laws + 160 specs + L4 probe (40% vs 3.2%, 12.5×) are citable, falsifiable science; EU AI Act raises compliance demand.

Deal: Open-source (MIT/CC-BY) + 5-year no-strings upgrade clause. Contact: GitHub Issues.
